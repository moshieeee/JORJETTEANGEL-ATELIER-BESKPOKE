The Atelier Method

Three Steps to Your Bespoke Garment

01

Book Consultation

One-on-one session with our master tailor. Select from hand-loomed piña-silk, high-twist Italian wools, and determine custom lapels, embroidery motifs, and silhouette specifications.

Session: 45 Minutes · Manila Atelier
02

The Basted Fitting

Experience our traditional basted fitting. The garment is temporarily stitched with white basting thread so our cutters can sculpt drape, armhole depth, and waist taper right onto your form.

Timeline: 5–7 Days Post-Consultation
03

Pick Up or Dispatch

Following meticulous hand-pressing, hand-stitched bar tacks, and final quality inspection, your garment is ready for collection in our salon or secure courier dispatch nationwide.
